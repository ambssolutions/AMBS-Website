Three steps, in order

How it works

1

We watch the work

We sit with whoever actually does the job. We leave with a written plan and an hours estimate. It is yours to keep, even if you don't hire us.

2

We build one automation first

Usually one to two weeks, depending on the job. You see it run on your real jobs, checked alongside the way you do it now, before it goes live.

3

You own it

We hand over the logins, train your team, and fix or tweak anything free for the first 30 days. If you stop working with us, the automation stays in your accounts.

No rip and replace

Built around the tools you already have

Most of the time you will not need new software. We start with what is already on your desk and your phone.
