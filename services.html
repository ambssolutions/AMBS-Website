What we actually do

Ambs Solutions is a small Auckland team that takes repetitive admin off New Zealand small businesses and builds the digital tools around it. Every job starts with the same question: what does your team do the same way every week? Quotes typed up from site notes, booking reminders sent by hand, the same customer questions answered ten times a day, invoices copied into Xero, the weekly numbers added up on a Sunday night. Those are the tasks we automate.

The digital side covers the tools a small business needs to run and to be found: a fast website that turns visitors into calls, a staff or customer portal, and a dashboard that shows the numbers without anyone building a spreadsheet.

Built on the tools you already pay for

We do not sell software. We connect and build on what you have: Xero, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, your booking tool and your website. Everything is set up in your own accounts, so you own the logins and it keeps working whether or not we are around. Where a tool really is the wrong one, we say so in the free session, and we tell you what we would use instead and why.

How we work with Auckland businesses

We work with businesses across Auckland, from the North Shore to Manukau and out to the West, and with businesses across New Zealand. The free first session can happen at your premises or online. Most of the build happens remotely, in New Zealand hours, and we come out when seeing the job in person makes a real difference, for example walking through a workshop, a clinic front desk or a café counter at opening time.

Auckland customers speak many languages. Websites, booking pages and customer replies can work in Chinese, Hindi, Punjabi and te reo Māori alongside English, and we train your team in English, Hindi or Punjabi.

What a project costs

We do not publish a price list, because the same task can be an afternoon in one business and a fortnight in another. After the free session you get a written plan with an hours estimate, yours to keep even if you go elsewhere. The scope and price are agreed in writing before any work starts, fixes and tweaks are free for the first 30 days, and support after that is a monthly plan or pay as you go.

Your data

Automations touch customer names, phone numbers and sometimes financial details. We handle them under the Privacy Act 2020, keep data we store for you in New Zealand or Australia, and never use it to train anyone's AI model. Our guide to the Privacy Act 2020 and automation sets out what to check before you start.

Common questions

Which service should I start with?

Start with the task that eats the most hours each week. For most businesses that is quotes and invoices, booking reminders or customer replies. The free session is where we work that out together, and you leave with a written plan whichever way you go.

Do you only work with Auckland businesses?

No. We are based in Auckland and work right across New Zealand. The free session and most of the build happen online, so a business in Hamilton, Wellington or Christchurch gets the same service as one in Albany.

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. We build on Xero, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and the booking or job tools you already use. If something genuinely needs a new tool, we say so and explain why.

How long does a project take?

Most first automations and websites take one to two weeks from the written plan to going live. Bigger builds like portals and dashboards are planned in stages, so something useful is working early.

What happens after the work is done?

Fixes and tweaks are free for the first 30 days. After that you can choose a monthly support plan or pay as you go. Because everything is built in your own accounts, you are never locked in.
