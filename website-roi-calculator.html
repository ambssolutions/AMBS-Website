Common questions

Are these real figures?

No. The sliders start with example numbers to show how the sum works. In a free session we look at your business and work out an estimate together.

Where do I find my website visitor numbers?

Google Analytics or Google Search Console shows how many people visit your site each month. If you do not have a website yet, start the visitors slider low.

What does the website cost slider mean?

Put in the price you have been quoted for a website. The chart shows the month when the work it brings in adds up to more than that price.

Does this work for my trade?

Yes. The sum is the same for any business that takes enquiries: builders, electricians, plumbers, cabinet makers, cafés, clinics and more. Change the job value to suit your work.
