Why the numbers get skipped

Every owner means to look at the numbers. The trouble is that looking means opening Xero, then a spreadsheet, then the booking tool, and adding things up in your head. On a busy week that gets pushed to the weekend, and on a busier one it gets skipped. Decisions get made on a feeling instead.

A dashboard that updates itself, and a short email that arrives before the week starts, turn that into a two-minute habit.

What we build

A live dashboard

One page, usually a tab in Google Sheets or Excel, that shows the numbers you chose, updated from Xero, your sheets and your booking tool without anyone typing. This week against last week, and against the same week last year once you have it. Opens on a phone.

A weekly numbers email

Monday morning, five or six numbers with a one-line note on each: "Sales $12,400, up 8% on last week. Three invoices overdue, $4,200. Eleven bookings this week, two unconfirmed." Short enough to read at the lights. It can go to Teams or Google Chat instead of email if that is where you look.

Alerts for the things that matter

A message when cash drops below a line you set, when an invoice goes 14 days overdue, or when a day's bookings are half empty. Only the ones you ask for.

Numbers we usually include

  • Sales and invoices issued this week, this month
  • Cash in the bank, and what is due in and out in the next two weeks
  • Invoices owing and overdue, with the biggest three named
  • Quotes sent, accepted and the win rate
  • Jobs or bookings ahead, and no-shows last week
  • Website enquiries and where they came from

How it works

  1. Free session. We ask what you look at now, what you wish you knew, and where each number lives.
  2. Written plan. The numbers, the sources, and what the dashboard and the email will look like. Yours to keep.
  3. Build and test. Usually one to two weeks, in your own accounts, checked against Xero so the numbers match.
  4. Refine. Fixes and tweaks are free for the first 30 days, while we add the number you realise you also want.

Your data and privacy

Dashboards show financial information, so access matters. The dashboard lives in your own account, you decide who can open it, and data we store for you is hosted in New Zealand or Australia under the Privacy Act 2020. It is never used to train anyone's AI model.
