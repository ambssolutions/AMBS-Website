The cost of an empty slot

A no-show is not just a lost hour. It is the customer who could have had that slot, the staff member paid to wait, and the phone calls spent trying to fill it. For a clinic, a salon or a tradesperson with a full week, a handful of no-shows a week adds up to a serious amount over a year.

Most no-shows are not rude. People forget, or something comes up and ringing feels awkward. A reminder with an easy way to say "yes, still coming" or "can I move it" fixes most of them.

What we set up

Online booking from your website

A booking page that shows real availability, with the gaps and buffers you need between jobs, and the right staff member or service. Bookings go straight into the calendar you already use: Google Calendar, Outlook or your booking tool's own.

Confirmations and reminders

A confirmation the moment someone books. A reminder by text or email the day before, with a one-tap confirm or reschedule link. An optional second nudge for anyone who has not confirmed. Everything in your own words and, where you want, your customers' languages.

Connected to the rest of your business

A completed appointment can become a draft invoice in Xero, a row in your client list and a follow-up message a week later. That is where the app connections come in.

Who this is for

  • Clinics and salons: physios, dentists, barbers, beauty and massage, where every slot counts
  • Trades: site visits, quotes and small jobs booked from the website, with a text when the van is on its way
  • Cleaning and home services: regular bookings with reminders and easy rescheduling
  • Advisers: consultations booked straight into your calendar with the details you need up front

How it works

  1. Free session. We look at how bookings arrive now, where no-shows happen and what tools you already have.
  2. Written plan. The booking flow, the reminder timings and wording, and what connects to what. Yours to keep.
  3. Build and test. Usually one to two weeks. We test it with real bookings before it goes live.
  4. Tune it. Fixes and tweaks are free for the first 30 days, while we adjust timings to what works for your customers.

Your data and privacy

Bookings carry names, phone numbers and sometimes health details. They are handled under the Privacy Act 2020, data we store for you is hosted in New Zealand or Australia, and it is never used to train anyone's AI model. Text messages go through providers that keep data in New Zealand or Australia where we have the choice.
