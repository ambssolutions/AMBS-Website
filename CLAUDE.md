# AMBS Solutions website notes

These notes apply to this site and to any other AMBS Solutions website.

## First thing in every chat: check for waiting translations
- Run `git fetch origin main`, merge it, then run `python3 site-src/pending_translations.py`.
- If anything is waiting, tell the owner in one line, for example "3 editor changes are waiting for translation".
- When they say "translate the editor changes":
  1. Write the Chinese, Hindi, Punjabi and te reo Māori translations into a JSON file.
  2. Run `python3 site-src/pending_translations.py --apply <file>`, then `python3 site-src/build.py`.
  3. Show the translations and wait for "push live".

## Working style
- Make changes locally and show screenshots or a video first.
- Publish to the live site only when the owner says "push live".

## Design preferences
- Stacked card sections (like "How it works"): use native CSS `position: sticky` stacking.
  - The section heading pins under the top bar (CSS sticky), so the whole section feels pinned.
  - Cards scroll normally and stick just below that heading, one under the next, each leaving its number row showing.
  - Then they release together.
  - Don't pin sections or move the cards with scroll-driven JavaScript. The owner wants the effortless, native feel.
- Expanding cards (like "What we do" on phones): unfold slowly and smoothly with a native CSS fold (`grid-template-rows` transition, about 1.4s), with the content fading in.
- Colours: use the logo's green for all greens, and brand blue and purple from the logo.
- Dark mode logos light up from grey to full colour with no glow or halo around them.
- Top bar: every item keeps the same size and place in every language (fixed widths sized for Māori, the longest: language box 132px on laptops / 105px on phones, menu pill 526px, Book a call 120–124px, Contact us 108–116px, words centred; `home.css` end). Its laptop column is 1240px (`padding-inline` on `header .bar`). Hero headings get more line space in Hindi/Punjabi (1.3), Chinese (1.3, smaller on phones so the full stop stays on its line) and Māori (1.16) so letters never run into the next line. Check with a script that switches all 5 languages and compares positions after any header change.

Tablets (761–1023px) show the settings and the preview side by side. Start, design and details screens return to editing with "✓ Done". Settings buttons and boxes are at least 44px tall with 16px text. A new signature goes start → details (while setting up, "Check details" only lists what the website scan found, with no ticks; found lines that weren't picked are kept but hidden) → "What do you want in your signature?" (worked out again from the details each time you move forward, so the two screens always agree) (`wants` step, drawn as clean grouped lists with switches and line icons (`lineIcon()`), social networks as a grid of their logos (`wantSocial()`): every part is its own tick, grouped as contact details (each line, `c_*` → `S.hideC`), social media (each network, `s_*` → `S.hideS`), buttons (booking, review, another; `b_*`) and pictures and extras (logo, photo, tagline, handwritten sign-off, banner, disclaimer); ticking any contact line, social network, button, tagline or disclaimer opens a box under it straight away to type or check its number, link or words (filled in when known, first empty box focused; `wantCard()`); `WANT`, `applyWants()` applied to every template through `presetLook`) → designs (matching ones first) → editing; "Change design" also asks first. On laptops (1100px+) the space under the preview shows the same signature live in the three other views of laptop/phone × light/dark, each scaled to fit its card ( `pvExtra()`, `pvRender()`, `pvDark()`), and tapping one switches to it. The preview shows only Laptop/Phone, Light/Dark, Full view and "⋯ More" (Even spacing, parts, move, test links, phone guide off by default, bigger preview). On phones "Move or add parts" is a sheet (`phoneMove()`) with "+ Add a part" (the `ADDABLE` parts not already showing; a button asks for its words and link first) above the Order list; the drag board is laptop only. Icon sets for social and contact icons include soft circles/squares, dark/grey squares and square/dark outlines (PNGs in `assets/email/<style>-<icon>[-eN].png`, made from `iconSvg()`). Social networks also include Pinterest, Threads, Snapchat, Telegram and Messenger (paths from simple-icons). Letter labels (M, P, E, W, A) have 22 colour sets (`SETS`) and per-letter colours (`S.letterCols`): each letter has a row of quick colour dots (brand colours first, then `QUICK`) plus a colour wheel for any colour; changing one letter after "One colour" keeps the others. "Order & layout" is one list of every part top to bottom (`allOrder()`: show/hide, drag or ▲▼, and one "where it goes" dropdown per part: beside your details, same line as the part above, underneath everything, and "Under the logo" when the logo is on the left or right (social icons through `S.socialWithLogo`; buttons, tagline and other parts through `S.underLogo`, drawn under the logo and its icons by `signatureInner()`/`ulIds()`; `placeChoices()`/`setPlace()`); every part's own settings also start with this "Where it goes" choice, and its ◀▶ arrows move it under the logo and back; the logo row picks its own position; on the "What do you want" screen it lists only the parts switched on; a hand-set order turns `autoFit` off; empty buttons stay in the order but aren't listed), then contact lines and social icons. The "What do you want in your signature?" screen has the same order list under the ticks; the order set there (`WANT_ORDER`, `applyOrder()`) is kept by every design. Each button (`book`, `review`, `extra`) has its own settings and its own look ("This button’s look": Filled/Outline/Text links, width, height, text size, corners, embossed in `S.btnOwn[id]`, drawn through `withBtn()`, falling back to the shared look through `getP`); only the gaps are shared. Changing a look setting on the Buttons panel sets it for every button (clears that setting from `S.btnOwn`). Choosing "same line as the part above" checks the phone width (`joinFitNote()`) and says so when the part drops underneath on phones for lack of room. Parts placed under the logo keep their own fixed gap and are left out of the column beside it when spacing (`UL` in `pass()`); nudges stop at what the layout can show (no part pulled above its line, ±400/600px at most). Even spacing also checks phone fit (makes a phone layout with `fitPhone2()` when too wide), Gmail size, pictures online, Outlook, and shows a "Spaced and checked" summary.
- **Server code:** it lives in `api/` (Vercel functions) and needs two Vercel environment variables: `GITHUB_TOKEN` (fine-grained, this repo only, Contents read and write) and `ADMIN_EMAILS` (`ADMIN_EMAIL` also works; the live project uses that name). Sign-in uses the AMBS Supabase project.
