# AMBS Solutions website notes

These notes apply to this site and to any other AMBS Solutions website.

## First thing in every chat: check for waiting translations
- Run `git fetch origin main`, merge it, then run `python3 site-src/pending_translations.py`.
- If anything is waiting, tell the owner in one line, for example "3 editor changes are waiting for translation".
- When they say "translate the editor changes":
  1. Write the Chinese, Hindi, Punjabi and te reo Māori translations into a JSON file.
  2. Run `python3 site-src/pending_translations.py --apply <file>`, then `python3 site-src/build.py`.
  3. Show the translations and wait for "push live".

## Working style
- Make changes locally and show screenshots or a video first.
- Publish to the live site only when the owner says "push live".

## Design preferences
- Stacked card sections (like "How it works"): use native CSS `position: sticky` stacking.
  - The section heading pins under the top bar (CSS sticky), so the whole section feels pinned.
  - Cards scroll normally and stick just below that heading, one under the next, each leaving its number row showing.
  - Then they release together.
  - Don't pin sections or move the cards with scroll-driven JavaScript. The owner wants the effortless, native feel.
- Expanding cards (like "What we do" on phones): unfold slowly and smoothly with a native CSS fold (`grid-template-rows` transition, about 1.4s), with the content fading in.
- Colours: use the logo's green for all greens, and brand blue and purple from the logo.
- Dark mode logos light up from grey to full colour with no glow or halo around them.

## Website editor (/admin)

  - guide, industry, service and legal pages: details, article text, photos, questions and related pages
  - new and deleted pages
  - the section-page intros and the service cards
  - photos, the logo and the sharing picture
  - phone, email and location, changed site-wide
  - the wording on every page other than the homepage (top bar, booking box, footer, page labels such as "Common questions", the guide calculator, and the 404 and booking-confirmed pages): Business details tab → "Wording on every page". It's saved in `site-src/content/site-text.json`; the labels are in `api/_sitetext.js`, and `build.py` reads it through `T()`/`TE()`. New fixed text in a page template goes there, never straight into the HTML.
  - Google Analytics: the measurement ID is `analytics_id` in `site-src/content/site.json` (Business details → Google Analytics). `build.py` adds the tag to every built page, `index.html` and `email-signature.html` (between `<!--ga-->` markers), never `/admin`. It sends `book_click`, `phone_click`, `email_click`, `generate_lead` (homepage form and form blocks) and `booking_confirmed` events.
  - "Search the whole site" finds all of this: homepage wording, every page and post, section intros, menus, the wording on every page and business details
  - the hours calculator
  - adding and removing homepage items: questions, Why us rows, What we do points, About us letters, How it works steps, trust points, the tools strip
  - What we do cards: new cards with a photo, removal, and swapping a drawing for a photo (original drawings are kept in `site-src/content/wd-drawings.json` so they can come back)
  - the app and browser icons
  - every change can be previewed at phone, tablet and laptop size before publishing
  - menus and footer links (`site-src/content/menus.json`)
  - homepage section order, hiding sections, and new sections made of blocks (`site-src/content/home-sections.json`)
  - pages at any address made of blocks (`site-src/content/custom/<slug>.json`) and News posts (section `news` in `pages.json`)
  - drafts (commits starting "Editor draft:" are saved but not built or deployed) and History with undo
  - scheduled publishing: a draft with `publish_at` (New Zealand time) goes live once that time passes. `editor-rebuild.yml` runs hourly, and `site-src/scheduled_due.py` decides whether a build is needed
  - contact form blocks: `site-src/assets/forms.js` sends each message through Web3Forms (the same key as the homepage form) and to portal Leads
  - News and Guides pages have a search box and category filters, and `/news?c=Category` opens filtered
  - drag-and-drop ordering (the ⠿ handle) for blocks, form questions, list items, menus and homepage sections
- **Anything new added in a chat must be editable in /admin too.**
  - New pages, posts, sections or wording go into the editor's data files (`pages.json`, `custom/`, `home-sections.json`, `menus.json`, the English list in `home.js`), not hard-coded into the HTML.
  - A new kind of content (for example a Blog or Case studies section) also gets added to `SECTIONS`/`LISTED` in `api/pages.js`, `SECTION` in `build.py`, and the "New page" choices and `SEC` names in `admin.html`, so the owner can add more of it.
  - Say in the reply how the owner will edit it.
- **How the editor saves:** each save commits straight to `main`.
  - Homepage wording, photos, the logo and calculator commits start with "Website editor:" and deploy directly.
  - Page and business-detail commits start with "Editor source:". Vercel skips those (`ignoreCommand` in `vercel.json`), and `.github/workflows/editor-rebuild.yml` then runs `build.py`, bumps `version.json` and commits, which deploys once.
- **Where the content lives:**
  - The page list, hub intros and service cards are in `site-src/content/pages.json`.
  - Business details are in `site-src/content/site.json`.
  - Uploaded photos are in `assets/uploads/`.
- **Before any change:** run `git fetch origin main` and merge it normally. Never use `-X ours`, or the owner's edits get overwritten. If wording conflicts, keep main's version.
- **Translations:** when the owner changes English in the editor, that line's other languages are removed (visitors see the English) and marked "needs" in `translation-status.json`. Claude translates them in a chat, as described at the top of this file. The editor has no translation service of its own, so there's no API cost.
- **Blocks:** `site-src/assets/blocks.mjs` draws every block, for the build (through `site-src/blocks-cli.mjs`, so the build needs node) and for the editor's preview (published copy at `/assets/blocks.mjs`). Styles are in `blocks.css`. Homepage block wording uses `ed.*` translation keys. The build records their English in `site-src/content/ed-strings.json` and queues changed lines in `translation-status.json`.
- **Homepage structure code:** adding and removing items is in `api/_home.js`; new wording is added to the English list in `home.js` and marked "needs" for translation.
- **Chat helper ("Ask us" on every page, introduces itself as "Ambs Bot"):** free, no AI service.
  - `site-src/build.py` gathers the site's own wording (homepage list in `home.js`, every live page and post with its questions, legal pages, block pages, business details) plus the owner's answers in `site-src/content/chatbot.json` into `assets/chat-index.json`, and adds `assets/chat.js` to every public page (between `<!--chat-->` markers; not `/admin` or `/email-signature`).
  - `chat.js` finds answers in the visitor's browser. It replies with the answer itself: the owner's answers and page questions in full, and for page text the most relevant sentences (`answerText()`; `build.py` ends headings, list items and paragraphs as sentences through `_txts()`), with a small "More on this:" link (label `more_on`) to the page it came from instead of page titles and lists of related pages. "What is an API / what does CRM mean" answers from the glossary (`define()`). Yes/no questions ("can you…", "do you…", "is it…") start with "Yes." (label `yes`, `yesLead()`) when the answer is a clear match, never for a page's own Q&A and never when the answer's first sentence says "not", "don't", "only", "unless"… so it never says yes to something the site says no to. "Can you / do you" questions prefer the owner's own answers, then the homepage cards (each industry's "What we'd take off you first"), then service and industry pages, when their question or title is about the thing asked (`asksAbout()`). Questions about the bot itself ("do you have any knowledge?") get the `knowledge` everyday reply; "do you speak Hindi?" shows the language buttons. When it can't answer it offers booking (`/#book`), a message (Web3Forms email + portal Leads, like the contact forms) and the phone and email.
  - The Chat helper tab shows one section at a time, picked from buttons along the top (`.chsub` in `admin.html`, `chSec()`): Settings (on/off, greeting, can't-find message, start-after-a-while), Visitor questions (badge = how many need an answer; three lists picked by buttons: "Not answered" = the helper couldn't answer and nobody has yet; "Answer added" = you or Claude wrote or linked an answer or marked it (`done`), each with a green "Next time the bot will answer:" note (`fix_note`, `fix_by`, `fix_at` columns; the editor saves it through `api/settings` `chatlogset` with `note` → `chat_questions_note`, the daily routine through the key-protected `chat_questions_resolve`), with "Move to Not answered"; "Answered by the bot" = the helper answered it, with "+ Add a better answer"; "Won't answer" = spam, gibberish or not about the business (`status` = 'skip', grey note with the reason; the owner's "Won't answer" button or the routine); a question checked by the routine that needs the owner's facts stays in "Not answered" with an amber "Needs your info" note (`status` = 'need'); "Move to Not answered" clears status and note (`chat_questions_mark`); `listOf()` in `admin.html`), Your answers (badge = count), Quick questions, Everyday replies and Window wording. Save & publish saves every section. The first time it opens in a visit it goes straight to Visitor questions when some need an answer; after that the last section used is remembered. "+ Add an answer" opens a box under the question: write the answer and "Save & publish answer", or under "Already answered?" pick one of up to 3 similar answers (`similar()`, by shared words) and "Use this answer" to add the question to its \"also\" wordings, or search all your answers under "Link it to another answer" (question matches first, then wordings, then answer text) and link it the same way; both mark the question done and publish. Each section shows one short line with the full help behind a "?" button. "Search the whole site" opens the section with the match.
  - Owner edits it in /admin → Chat helper tab (`api/settings` actions `chatget`/`chatbot`, "Editor source: chat helper" commits): on/off, greeting, quick questions, their own answers, everyday-conversation replies (how are you, are you a person, thanks, bye, off-topic…), the "offer help after a while" message and delay (`nudge`, passed on the script tag) and the chat window wording. All its wording (greeting, quick questions, own answers, everyday replies, window wording) goes through the normal translation queue as `ed.chat.*` keys, so editing English queues it for Claude; the index carries the homepage and those answers in every language (`g` = language) and the chat answers in the page's language or the script the visitor types (Gurmukhi → Punjabi, Devanagari → Hindi, Chinese characters → Chinese). It also recognises Punjabi, Hindi, te reo Māori and Chinese typed in English letters ("kinna kharcha hai", "aap kya karte ho", "e hia te utu", "nihao"): `romanLang()`/`romanMeaning()` in `chat.js` pick the language and turn the words into search words, and translated answers share an id (`i` in the index) so an answer found in English is shown in that language. Under the greeting a line (label `langs_line`, `{langs}` becomes the other languages' names) and a row of language buttons (English · 中文 · हिन्दी · ਪੰਜਾਬੀ · te reo Māori) show the chat speaks every language; a button switches the chat's language and greets again in it. A "Reply in English" button (label `in_english`, always in English, not translated) follows each such answer. "Kia ora" on its own doesn't switch to Māori. Guide and service pages answer in English with a note.
  - It starts the conversation after 15 seconds on a page (`nudge`: the window opens, on phones as a short "peek" window that opens fully when tapped), once per visit; once someone closes it, it stays closed while they click around (sessionStorage `ambs:chat-quiet`). A new visit (website opened again from a link, bookmark, search or typed address, a reload, or back after 30+ minutes away, including a phone tab resumed without reloading; `newVisit()` in `chat.js`) clears that and the old conversation, so it starts again; `?chat=reset` clears it for testing. A microphone button uses the browser's speech recognition (hidden where unsupported). "How good are you / why choose you" questions are answered from the homepage Why us list and promises (`why` in the index), in the chat's language.
  - Matching (`search()`/`answer()` in `chat.js`): coverage is weighted by how rare each word is; the question's key word (its rarest known word, not who it's for: "a website for a real estate agent") must be in the answer; an owner's answer must have it in its question or "also" wordings (`qaAbout()`); typing an owner's question or one of its wordings exactly always picks that answer; spelling is corrected to the website's own words (`spell()`); "doesn't/don't" are kept as "not"; "can you / do you…" questions are answered only by an owner's answer, a homepage card or a service or industry page, never a guide sentence; cost questions go to an owner's answer about cost. "Yes." is added only to yes/no questions (also "…, can you help?"), never to "X or Y?" questions, and an owner's answer written "Yes. …" loses the "Yes." for other questions. A question about the business it can't answer gets `not_found` (book, message, call); `offtopic` is only for questions with nothing about us or the visitor's business. Test lists (272 researched questions, 40 held-out) are in the scratchpad `chat/` folder of the chat that built them; re-run before and after any change to these rules.
  - Punjabi and Hindi typed in English letters: `ROMAN` in `chat.js` maps about 250 everyday words, including phone spellings like "kr", "skde", "nwi", "chahidi", to English search words. `RPHRASE` maps phrases ("nahi aunde" = no-show, "paise wapis" = refund, "kinne din" = how long). `RSTOP` drops filler words ("doge", "plz", "veer", "mera"). `RW` words decide whether a message is Punjabi or Hindi. "Can you…" with nothing else asked ("ki kam kr skde ho?") means "what do you do". For these messages an owner's answer close behind beats a page's sentence, and they never get the off-topic reply. "Sat Shrine akal" (autocorrect) and other spellings are the Punjabi greeting (`fixGreet()`). A place name after "in/at/from/near" that the website never uses ("in Otahuhu") is left out of the search. A test set of 200 such questions is in the scratchpad `chat/round2.json` of the chat that built it.
  - Industries (`IND`, `INDC`, `indCard()`, `indStrip()` in `chat.js`): a business the site doesn't name is read as the nearest industry (dentist, physio, barber → clinic; bakery, restaurant, food truck → café; roofer, mechanic, sparky → trades; pharmacy, florist, bottle store → shop; lawyer, broker, bookkeeper → adviser; courier, removals, taxi → transport); one with no industry page (gym, church, charity, school…) becomes "industry" and gets the industries answer, or is dropped when something else is asked ("church, can you help with our website?" → the website answer). A business named with nothing else asked ("I run a physio clinic, how can you help?", "Do you work with dentists?") gets that industry's homepage card ("What we'd take off you first"), also when the card lists the task asked ("plumber, quotes?"); otherwise the business is left out and the task is answered ("mechanic, can you send booking reminders?" → reminders). "Are you a…" and "come to the shop" don't count. Test lists: `industry.json` (68) and `industry2.json` (24, held out) in the scratchpad `chat/` folder.
  - More matching rules: one of the owner's wordings appearing word for word inside a longer message picks that answer (`altSeq`); an owner's answer level with a page's text wins; a cost question rerouted to the price answer counts as a clear match; numbers in a question weigh little. Polite refusals (`offtopic`) for repairs ("fix my laptop", "printer theek kar doge"), essays, "is Xero down", "book a plumber" and another shop's hours; sales pitches ("offer you our SEO services", "can we partner") get the contact details; AI receptionists / answering the phone and "buy my business" get `not_found`. The scratchpad graders (`grade.py`, `grade2.py`) score 272/272, 200/200, 250/250 and 40/40.
  - Daily routine "Daily chat answers" (claude.ai Routines, 7:50am NZ): reads unanswered questions through the key-protected Supabase function `chat_questions_todo` (`chat_questions_pending` is the older, shorter version) (key in `private.chat_digest_key`, never in the repo), prepares answers from the site's own facts on branch `daily-chat-answers` as a draft PR, moves each answered question to "Answer added" with its next-time note (`chat_questions_resolve`), marks the rest "Needs your info" or "Won't answer" with a note (`chat_questions_flag`), and sends a summary. Nothing goes live without "push live".
  - After an answer it shows "People also ask:" (label `also_ask`): up to 3 of the owner's own questions on the same topic, in the chat's language, not ones already asked (`related()` in `chat.js`; on/off is `related` in `chatbot.json`, /admin → Chat helper).
  - Questions visitors type are saved (email addresses and phone numbers masked; not from localhost unless `?chat=log`; `?chat=nolog` skips) through the Supabase function `chat_log` into table `chat_questions` (AMBS Supabase project, RLS on, no direct access). /admin → Chat helper → "Questions visitors asked" lists them through `api/settings` actions `chatlog`/`chatlogset`, which call `chat_questions_list`/`chat_questions_update` as the signed-in person (allowed only for active admins in `portal_users`). "Add an answer" copies a question into "Your own answers". On/off is `log` in `chatbot.json` (build passes the address to the index as `bot.log`). The privacy policy says so (section 2, "Chat helper").
  - Homepage wording edits publish without a rebuild, so `scheduled_due.py` rebuilds within the hour when `home.js` no longer matches the index.
